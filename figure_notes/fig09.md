# Figure 9. One-sided azimuthal sampling of the high-amplitude sector

- **Official caption:** Azimuthal evidence. (a) Station azimuth coverage: no station exists between 45° and 180°; red lines mark the three stations that set the first magnitude. ---

## English

### What the figure shows

Four panels on **azimuth relative to the Taitung epicentre**.

**(a)** Polar histogram of 166 P-file stations. A large sector **45–180° has no stations** (sea). Red rays: the three first-alert magnitude stations, packed in ~295–307° (a 12° sector).

**(b)** Heat map of median single-station \(M_{Pd}\) by azimuth sector × distance band. The cell used by the first report — **WNW, < 60 km** — is the **hottest** (median 6.54). Distant bands (150–400 km) sit nearer 5.4–5.9 but join only after the eleventh report.

**(c)** Polar scatter of single-station magnitude residual vs azimuth. First-report stations (stars) sit where residuals are most positive. A one-cycle harmonic peaks near the landward WNW azimuth.

**(d)** Mean direction of strongest distance-corrected PGA by distance band, pointing **northwest** (~314–328°), i.e. toward the land network.

### How to read it

This is **not** “gap = 300° therefore quality is poor.” Gap is large for *any* correct offshore location. The damaging fact is that the **first \(n_m=3\) stations occupy 12° on the strong side**. LDU (nearest, azimuth 292°) failed \(Pd\) integration and could not pull the mean down.

Azimuthal gap as a gate is **spurious** when the epicentre is placed inland: the six smallest-gap solutions are the six worst-located (mean 41 km error).

### Scientific meaning

**Bias term 2 (one-sided sampling).** Early \(Pd\) averages one radiation lobe, not the focal sphere. A nodal first sector would have **under**-estimated (missed warning risk). The proposed gate is **azimuthal spread of magnitude stations**, not the classical gap around the epicentre.

## 中文

### 圖面內容

相對臺東震央的方位四聯圖。

**(a)** 166 站極座標直方圖。**45–180° 無站**（海域）。紅線：首報規模三站，擠在約 295–307°（僅 12°）。

**(b)** 方位扇區 × 距離帶的單站 \(M_{Pd}\) 中位熱圖。首報用的 **WNW、< 60 km** 格是**最熱的**（中位 6.54）。遠帶 5.4–5.9，要到第 11 報以後才進解。

**(c)** 單站規模殘差對方位。首報站（星）落在殘差最正的一側。

**(d)** 距離改正後 PGA 最強方向指向**西北**（約 314–328°），也就是陸地網方向。

### 如何判讀

重點不是「缺口 300°＝品質差」——正確外海解缺口本來就大。真正有害的是**首報 \(n_m=3\) 只佔強振幅側 12°**。最近站 LDU（方位 292°）\(Pd\) 積分失敗，無法把平均拉下來。震央被放到陸上時，缺口會**假變小**：缺口最小的 6 筆解，定位最差（平均誤差 41 km）。

### 科學意義

高估的**第二項：單側取樣**。早期 \(Pd\) 平均的是一個輻射瓣，不是整個焦球。若首批站在節面，會**低估**（漏報風險）。建議閘門是**計算規模所用測站的方位展度**，不是震央週圍的古典缺口。
