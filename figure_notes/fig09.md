# Figure 9. One-sided azimuthal sampling of the high-amplitude sector

- **Image:** `fig09.png`
- **Section:** 5
- **Official caption:** Azimuthal evidence. (a) Station azimuth coverage: no station exists between 45° and 180°; red lines mark the three stations that set the first magnitude. (b) Median single-station magnitude by azimuth sector and distance band. (c) Azimuth versus single-station magnitude residual for the final solution. (d) Direction of the strongest distance-corrected observed PGA per distance band.

## English

### What is drawn

Four panels. The PNG title states the inference: the one-sided geometry sampled the **strong-amplitude azimuth**.

**(a)** Polar histogram of 166 P-file stations around the Taitung epicentre. A shaded sector **45–180° has no stations** (open sea). Three **red rays** mark the first-alert magnitude stations, packed at 295–307° (a **12°** sector, WNW). Station density is highest NNW.

**(b)** Heat map of median single-station \(M_{Pd}\) by azimuth sector × distance band. The cell used by the first report — **WNW (270–300°), 0–60 km** — is hot: median **6.54** (\(n=8\)). A North 0–60 km cell reaches 6.82. Distant bands (150–400 km) sit nearer 5.4–5.9 but join only after the eleventh report.

**(c)** Polar scatter of single-station magnitude residual (\(M_{Pd}-5.85\)) vs azimuth. Yellow stars = first-report stations, on the positive (overestimate) side. A one-cycle harmonic peaks near **282°** with amplitude **+0.42**. Red (positive) residuals cluster west/northwest; blue (negative) toward north and southwest.

**(d)** Mean direction of strongest distance-corrected PGA by distance band: all three vectors point **northwest (~314–328°)**, i.e. toward the land network.

### How to read it

This is **not** “gap = 300° therefore quality is poor.” Gap is large for *any* correct offshore location. The damaging fact is that the first \(n_m=3\) stations occupy 12° **on the strong side**. LDU (nearest, 14.1 km, azimuth 292°) recorded \(Pa\) = 162.3 gal but \(Pd\) = 0.0006 cm (\(M_{Pd}\) 3.46) — an integration/saturation failure that removed the only near-source constraint that could have pulled the mean down.

Azimuthal gap as a gate is **spurious** when the epicentre is placed inland: the six smallest-gap solutions (125–139°) are the six worst-located (mean 41 km), because an inland epicentre artificially improves apparent coverage. Among well-located solutions gap correlates only weakly with magnitude (\(r=0.23\)).

### Scientific meaning

**Bias term 2 (one-sided sampling).** Early \(M_{Pd}\) averages one radiation lobe, not the focal sphere. Here that lobe was the P-axis maximum (Fig. 10), so the bias is high. A nodal first sector (azimuth 210°, \(|F_P|=0.01\)) would have **under**-estimated — missed-warning risk. The proposed gate is **azimuthal spread of the stations used for magnitude**, not the classical gap around the epicentre.

### Common misreading

Panel (c)’s harmonic amplitude +0.42 at 282° is the *azimuthal residual pattern among stations*, not the same number as the +0.42 *relation bias* in Fig. 11. Those two 0.42s are different terms. The Fig. 11 +0.42 is what remains after geometry is good; the Fig. 9 +0.42 is a harmonic fit to single-station residuals.

## 中文

### 圖面內容

四聯圖。PNG 標題：單側幾何採到了**強振幅方位**。

**(a)** 相對臺東震央的 166 站極座標直方圖。著色扇區 **45–180° 無站**（開闊海域）。三條**紅線**是首報規模站，擠在 295–307°（僅 **12°**，WNW）。測站密度最高在 NNW。

**(b)** 方位扇區 × 距離帶的單站 \(M_{Pd}\) 中位熱圖。首報用的 **WNW（270–300°）、0–60 km** 格是熱的：中位 **6.54**（\(n=8\)）。北向 0–60 km 可到 6.82。遠帶 5.4–5.9，要到第 11 報以後才進解。

**(c)** 單站規模殘差（\(M_{Pd}-5.85\)）對方位。黃星＝首報站，落在正殘差（高估）側。一週諧波峰值約 **282°**、振幅 **+0.42**。正殘差（紅）集中西／西北，負殘差（藍）偏北與西南。

**(d)** 距離改正後 PGA 最強方向：三個距離帶的向量都指向**西北（約 314–328°）**，也就是陸地網方向。

### 如何判讀

重點不是「缺口 300°＝品質差」——正確外海解缺口本來就大。真正有害的是首報 \(n_m=3\) 只佔強振幅側 **12°**。最近站 LDU（14.1 km、方位 292°）\(Pa\)＝162.3 gal 但 \(Pd\)＝0.0006 cm（\(M_{Pd}\) 3.46）——積分／飽和失敗，拿掉唯一能把平均拉下來的近源約束。

震央被放到陸上時，缺口會**假變小**：缺口最小的 6 筆解（125–139°）定位最差（平均誤差 41 km）。定位好的解裡，缺口與規模幾乎無關（\(r=0.23\)）。

### 科學意義

高估的**第二項：單側取樣**。早期 \(M_{Pd}\) 平均的是一個輻射瓣，不是整個焦球。這裡落在 P 軸極大（圖 10），所以是高估。若首批站在節面（方位 210°，\(|F_P|=0.01\)）會**低估**——漏報風險。建議閘門是**計算規模所用測站的方位展度**，不是震央週圍的古典缺口。

### 容易讀錯的地方

(c) 諧波振幅 +0.42（282°）是*各站殘差的方位結構*，**不是**圖 11 裡路徑關係的 +0.42。兩個 0.42 是不同項：圖 11 的 +0.42 是幾何已經很好之後仍留下的；圖 9 的 +0.42 是單站殘差的諧波擬合。
