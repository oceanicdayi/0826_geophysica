# Figure 7. Per-second intensity heatmap by latitude (blind zone)

- **Image:** `fig07.png`
- **Section:** 4
- **Official caption:** Per-second maximum observed intensity by latitude band, with the alert times superimposed. Intensity 5 is reached in the 22.6°N band within about 4 s, well before the first alert at 13.7 s.

---

## English

### What the figure shows

A heatmap: **x** = seconds after origin (~0–120 s), **y** = latitude bins (~22–25°N), colour = maximum observed intensity in that bin and second (yellow low → red high). Vertical dashed lines mark PWS / first / second alert (~13.7–15 s on the plot; manuscript 13.67 s).

### How to read it

The **red patch near 22.6°N at ~4–13 s** is Green Island and the Taitung coast: intensity 4–5 **before** the alert lines. North of ~23.5°N the colour stays low until well after the vertical lines: those latitudes had tens of seconds of lead. Using 626 stations at 1 s sampling, the first issued solution preceded S-wave arrival at 99.0% of stations (median 33.6 s) and preceded intensity 3 at **92.5%** of stations that eventually reached 3 (median 29.1 s). Taitung City (~22.75°N, 48.7 km) has only ~2.1 s theoretical S lead after 13.67 s.

### Scientific meaning

Blind zone is **intrinsic** for an offshore source: the closest exposed community is the least protected. Population in that zone is small, so societal impact of the miss is limited, but the geometry cannot be removed by software gates. The heatmap is the time–space proof behind the 92.5% statistic.

## 中文

### 圖面內容

熱圖：橫軸發震後秒、縱軸緯度帶、顏色為該秒該帶最大實測震度。垂直虛線為 PWS／第 1、2 報（圖上約 14–15 秒；正文 13.67 秒）。

### 如何判讀

**約 22.6°N、4–13 秒的紅色**是綠島與臺東沿岸：警報線之前已達震度 4–5。約 23.5°N 以北，顏色在垂直線之後才升高，領先可達數十秒。626 站每秒資料：首報早於 99.0% 測站的 S 波（中位 33.6 s），早於最終達震度 3 之測站的 **92.5%**（中位 29.1 s）。臺東市理論 S 波領先僅約 2.1 秒。

### 科學意義

外海事件的盲區是**幾何內建的**：最近的暴露聚落也是最沒保護的。該區人口少，社會衝擊有限，但軟體閘門消不掉這塊。這張圖是 92.5% 統計背後的時空證據。
