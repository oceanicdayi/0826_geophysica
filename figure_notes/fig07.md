# Figure 7. Per-second intensity heatmap by latitude (blind zone)

- **Image:** `fig07.png`
- **Section:** 4
- **Official caption:** Per-second maximum observed intensity by latitude band, with the alert times superimposed. Intensity 5 is reached in the 22.6°N band within about 4 s, well before the first alert at 13.7 s.

---

## English

### What is drawn

A heatmap of 626 stations at 1 s sampling. **x** = seconds after origin (0–119 s, origin 07:00:11 UTC). **y** = latitude bins (~21.9–25.2°N). Colour = maximum observed intensity in that bin and second (pale yellow low → dark red intensity 5–6). Vertical dashed lines mark PWS / first / second alert. On the PNG those lines sit near **14 s** (manuscript value 13.67 s; the 0.3 s difference is rounding of the plotted timestamp).

### How to read it

The **dark-red patch near 22.6°N at ~4–13 s** is Green Island and the Taitung coast: intensity 4–5 **before** the alert lines. That is the blind zone in time–latitude. Colour then spreads **northward**: intensity 3–4 reaches ~24.5°N near 60 s. A persistent band near **25.0°N** (Taipei basin latitudes) shows prolonged intensity 2–3 after ~10 s, consistent with basin resonance, not with a second source.

Using these data, the first issued solution preceded S-wave arrival at 99.0% of stations (median lead 33.6 s) and preceded intensity 3 at **92.5%** of stations that eventually reached 3 (median 29.1 s). Taitung City (~22.75°N, 48.7 km) has only ~2.1 s theoretical S lead after the 13.67 s alert.

### Scientific meaning

The blind zone is **intrinsic** for an offshore east-coast source: the closest exposed community is the least protected. Population there is small, so the societal miss is limited, but no quality gate can create lead time inside the 0 s ring of Fig. 2. North of ~23.5°N the heatmap is the visual proof of the 92.5% statistic: those latitudes had tens of seconds after the vertical lines before strong colour arrives.

### Common misreading

The 25°N persistent band is not an early alert failure in Taipei; intensity there stays 2–3, and those counties were outside the PWS yellow of Fig. 2. Also, “intensity 5 at 4 s” is a *latitude-bin maximum*, driven by Green Island (LDU, 150.1 gal, intensity 4 in the analyst set; the heatmap uses per-second maxima across 626 stations).

## 中文

### 圖面內容

626 站每秒資料的熱圖。橫軸為發震後秒（0–119 s，原時 07:00:11 UTC），縱軸為緯度帶（約 21.9–25.2°N），顏色為該秒該帶最大實測震度（淺黃低 → 深紅 5–6 級）。垂直虛線為 PWS／第 1、2 報。PNG 上約在 **14 秒**（正文 13.67 秒；差 0.3 秒是繪圖時間戳取整）。

### 如何判讀

**約 22.6°N、4–13 秒的深紅塊**是綠島與臺東沿岸：警報線之前已達震度 4–5。這就是時間–緯度上的盲區。顏色隨後**向北**擴展：震度 3–4 約 60 秒到 24.5°N。**25.0°N** 附近有一條持續的 2–3 級帶（臺北盆地緯度），符合盆地共振，不是第二個震源。

這套資料：首報早於 99.0% 測站的 S 波（中位 33.6 s），早於最終達震度 3 之測站的 **92.5%**（中位 29.1 s）。臺東市理論 S 波領先僅約 2.1 秒。

### 科學意義

外海東岸事件的盲區是**幾何內建的**：最近的暴露聚落也是最沒保護的。該區人口少，社會衝擊有限，但任何品質閘門都無法在圖 2 的 0 秒圓內變出領先時間。約 23.5°N 以北，熱圖就是 92.5% 統計的視覺版：垂直線之後還有數十秒，顏色才升高。

### 容易讀錯的地方

25°N 的持續帶不是臺北「預警失敗」：那裡震度停在 2–3，且那些縣市不在圖 2 的 PWS 黃色裡。「4 秒就達震度 5」是*緯度帶最大值*，主要由綠島驅動（LDU 150.1 gal；熱圖用 626 站每秒最大）。
