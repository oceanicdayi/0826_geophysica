# Figure 8. Reported \(M_{Pd}\) tracks assumed hypocentral distance

- **Image:** `fig08.png`
- **Section:** 5
- **Official caption:** Evidence that reported magnitude tracks assumed distance. (a) Two solutions computed in the same second from identical \(Pd\) values. (b) Magnitude and location error report by report for a geometric-centre stream. (c) All 83 solutions, \(r=-0.74\). (d) Recovered operational relation, 2065 single-station records, rms 0.13.

---

## English

### What is drawn

Four panels. The PNG title states the inference: pulling the epicentre toward the stations **lowers** \(M_{Pd}\).

**(a)** Bar chart, four stations (G035, G036, G047, G046), same second, **identical \(Pd\)**. Red = Geiger (offshore \(R\) ~54–60 km) → station \(M_{Pd}\) 6.23–6.63, event **6.55**. Blue = geometric-centre (inland \(R\) ~15–21 km) → 5.43–5.67, event **5.66**. Dashed line at \(M_L\) 5.85. Difference 0.89 ≈ \(1.85\log_{10}(60/21)=0.84\) (Table 3).

**(b)** One geometric-centre stream, report by report: light bars = location error (~40 km then collapse); red line = \(M_{Pd}\). An annotation marks **location fixed → magnitude jumps** (5.46 → 6.42). When the epicentre later drifts inland, \(M_{Pd}\) falls again.

**(c)** Scatter of all 83 solutions: epicentral error vs \(M_{Pd}\), \(r=-0.74\), slope **−0.15 units per 10 km**. The regression crosses 5.85 near **~30 km error**.

**(d)** Predicted vs reported single-station \(M_{Pd}\) on the 1:1 line:  
\(M_{Pd}=4.79+1.16\log_{10}(Pd)+1.85\log_{10}(R)\), \(n=2065\), rms 0.13. \(Pd\) in cm, \(R\) in km.

### How to read it

Panel (d) is the key: reported \(M_{Pd}\) is a **deterministic function** of \(Pd\) and assumed \(R\), not an independent estimate. Therefore two solutions with the same \(Pd\) can differ only through \(R\). The **apparently good** 5.66 in (a) is the **wrong location**. The correctly located Geiger solution overestimates. Ranking by magnitude error alone would promote the worst epicentres. In (c), the solutions that match \(M_L\) 5.85 sit at ~30 km error: accuracy in magnitude is bought by inaccuracy in location.

### Scientific meaning

This is **bias term 1 (distance)**. Offshore mislocation is toward the network → smaller \(R\) → smaller \(M_{Pd}\). Fixing location **raises** \(M_{Pd}\) into the overestimate set by terms 2 and 3 (Fig. 11). Quality control must score location and magnitude **jointly**. A factor-of-two error in \(R\) costs 0.56 magnitude units because the \(\log_{10}R\) coefficient is 1.85.

### Common misreading

Do not conclude “the centroid stream was more accurate because 5.66 ≈ 5.85.” It was 35 km inland. Do not conclude that better location will recover 5.85; after location is fixed, \(M_{Pd}\) plateaus at 6.1–6.4 (Fig. 5a).

## 中文

### 圖面內容

四聯圖。PNG 標題已寫推論：把震央往測站拉會**壓低** \(M_{Pd}\)。

**(a)** 四站（G035、G036、G047、G046）柱狀圖：同一秒、**相同 \(Pd\)**。紅＝Geiger（外海 \(R\) 約 54–60 km）事件規模 **6.55**；藍＝幾何中心（陸上 \(R\) 約 15–21 km）**5.66**。虛線 \(M_L\) 5.85。差 0.89 ≈ \(1.85\log_{10}(60/21)=0.84\)（表 3）。

**(b)** 一條幾何中心串流逐報：淺色柱為定位誤差（約 40 km 後驟降），紅線為 \(M_{Pd}\)。標註 **定位一改對 → 規模跳升**（5.46→6.42）。震央再漂回陸上，規模又掉。

**(c)** 全部 83 筆解散點：震央誤差對 \(M_{Pd}\)，\(r=-0.74\)，斜率每 10 km **−0.15**。迴歸在誤差約 **30 km** 穿過 5.85。

**(d)** 單站預測對上報值落在 1:1 線：\(M_{Pd}=4.79+1.16\log_{10}(Pd)+1.85\log_{10}(R)\)，2065 筆，rms 0.13。\(Pd\) 單位 cm，\(R\) 單位 km。

### 如何判讀

(d) 是關鍵：上報的 \(M_{Pd}\) 是 \(Pd\) 與假設 \(R\) 的**確定函數**，不是獨立估計。因此相同 \(Pd\) 的兩筆解只能因 \(R\) 而不同。(a) 裡看起來準的 5.66 **是錯位置**。位置對的 Geiger 反而高估。只靠規模誤差排序，會選到最差震央。(c) 裡對上 5.85 的解，誤差約 30 km：規模準是用定位錯換來的。

### 科學意義

這是高估的**第一項：距離**。外海錯位朝向測站網 → \(R\) 變小 → \(M_{Pd}\) 變小。位置一改對，\(M_{Pd}\) 升高，再疊上輻射與路徑項（圖 11）。品質管制必須**定位與規模一起看**。\(R\) 差兩倍會造成 0.56 個規模單位，因為 \(\log_{10}R\) 係數是 1.85。

### 容易讀錯的地方

不要因為 5.66≈5.85 就說重心串流比較準——它在陸上 35 km。也不要以為定位改對就會回到 5.85；位置固定後 \(M_{Pd}\) 停在 6.1–6.4（圖 5a）。
