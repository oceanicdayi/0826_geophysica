## English

### What the figure shows (reconstructed; PNG absent)

Four panels that test whether the azimuthal \(Pd\)/PGA pattern is **radiation**, not rupture **directivity**. Coefficients follow Aki and Richards (2002, Box 4.4) from the RMT mechanism (Lee et al. 2014; strike 182°, dip 50°, rake 60°). \(|F|\) is floored at 0.10. \(|F_S|=\sqrt{F_{SV}^2+F_{SH}^2}\) is compared with PGA; \(|F_P|\) with \(Pd\).

**(a)** Lower-hemisphere P-wave radiation. Grey dots: 166 P-file stations. Red stars: the three first-magnitude stations, next to the **P axis** (azimuth 293°, plunge ~1°).

| Station | Azimuth | Take-off | \(|F_P|\) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| G036 | 295° | 68° | 0.748 |
| G047 | 297° | 70° | 0.790 |
| G035 | 307° | 65° | 0.559 |
| Mean of the three | | | **0.699** |
| All-azimuth average | | | 0.392 |

Ratio 0.699/0.392 = 1.78 → predicted \(\Delta M=1.16\log_{10}(1.78)=+0.29\) (Table 4). The 1.16 is the \(Pd\) coefficient in the operational relation of Fig. 8d.

**(b)** \(|F_P|\) and \(|F_S|\) versus azimuth; outer ticks show the empty 45–180° sector (same hole as Fig. 9a).

**(c)** Distance-corrected observed PGA versus theoretical \(|F_S|\). Univariate slope **+1.11**; multivariate exponent **+1.13** (theoretical 1.0; permutation \(p<0.001\)).

**(d)** Azimuthal residual harmonic amplitude drops from 0.453 to **0.028** \(\log_{10}\) units after removing distance and radiation (\(p=0.92\)): **no leftover directivity** in the sampled azimuth range. Expected for an \(M_L\) 5.85 rupture (~4–5 km, ~2 s) inside a 3 s \(Pd\) window.

Direct regression of report-file \(Pd\) on \(|F_P|\) is weak (\(p=0.123\)); the statistical case for radiation therefore rests on **S-wave/PGA**, plus the +0.29 versus +0.28 match with the geometry penalty.

### Scientific meaning

**Bias term 2 is radiation-pattern sampling**, quantified. Directivity is not required and is not detected. The +0.28 “geometry penalty” in Fig. 11 is the same number as this prediction. An east-southeast directivity lobe could not be seen: there are no stations between 45° and 180°.

### Common misreading

A good PGA–\(|F_S|\) fit is not a claim that \(Pd\) itself was regressed successfully on \(|F_P|\). The paper is explicit that the \(Pd\)–\(|F_P|\) regression is weak; the operational link is the predicted +0.29 from the three first-alert stations’ \(|F_P|\) ratio.

## 中文

### 圖面內容（依稿件重建；根目錄無 `fig10.png`）

四聯圖檢驗方位結構是**輻射圖案**還是破裂**方向性**。係數依 Aki and Richards (2002) 由 RMT 機制計算（Lee et al. 2014；182°/50°/60°）。\(|F|\) 下限 0.10。PGA 對 \(|F_S|\)，\(Pd\) 對 \(|F_P|\)。

**(a)** 下半球 P 波輻射。灰點 166 站；紅星為首報三站，緊貼 **P 軸**（方位 293°、傾伏約 1°）。三站平均 \(|F_P|=0.699\)，全方位平均 0.392，比值 1.78 → 預測規模偏差 \(+0.29\)（表 4）。1.16 即圖 8d 作業關係的 \(Pd\) 係數。

**(b)** \(|F_P|\)、\(|F_S|\) 對方位；外側刻度顯示 45–180° 無站（與圖 9a 同一缺口）。

**(c)** 距離改正 PGA 對理論 \(|F_S|\)。單變數斜率 **+1.11**，多變數指數 **+1.13**（理論 1.0；置換 \(p<0.001\)）。

**(d)** 去掉距離與輻射後，方位諧波振幅由 0.453 降到 **0.028**（\(p=0.92\)）：在已取樣方位內**沒有殘餘方向性**。對 \(M_L\) 5.85、3 秒 \(Pd\) 窗是合理的（破裂約 4–5 km、約 2 秒）。

報告檔 \(Pd\) 對 \(|F_P|\) 迴歸不顯著（\(p=0.123\)），輻射控制的統計證據主要在 **PGA／S 波**，再加上 +0.29 與 +0.28 吻合。

### 科學意義

**第二項偏差＝輻射圖案取樣**，可定量。不需要、也沒測到破裂方向性。圖 11 的 +0.28 幾何懲罰就是這個預測。東南東的方向性瓣即使存在也看不到：45–180° 沒有測站。

### 容易讀錯的地方

PGA 對 \(|F_S|\) 擬合得好，並不表示 \(Pd\) 對 \(|F_P|\) 迴歸成功。正文寫明後者不顯著；作業上的連結是首報三站 \(|F_P|\) 比值所預測的 +0.29。
