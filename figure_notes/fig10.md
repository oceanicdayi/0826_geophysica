## English

### What the figure shows (from the manuscript)

Four panels that test whether the azimuthal \(Pd\)/PGA pattern is **radiation**, not rupture **directivity**.

**(a)** Lower-hemisphere P-wave radiation from the RMT mechanism (Lee et al. 2014; strike 182°, dip 50°, rake 60°). Grey dots: 166 P-file stations. Red stars: the three first-magnitude stations, next to the **P axis** (azimuth 293°, plunge ~1°). Their mean \(|F_P|=0.699\) vs all-azimuth average 0.392 (ratio 1.78 → predicted \(\Delta M=1.16\log_{10}(1.78)=+0.29\); Table 4).

**(b)** \(|F_P|\) and \(|F_S|\) versus azimuth; ticks show the empty 45–180° sector.

**(c)** Distance-corrected observed PGA vs theoretical \(|F_S|\). Fitted exponent **+1.13** (theoretical 1.0; permutation \(p<0.001\)). A mechanism-free harmonic explains less of the rms.

**(d)** Azimuthal residual harmonic amplitude drops from 0.453 to **0.028** \(\log_{10}\) units after removing distance and radiation (\(p=0.92\)): **no leftover directivity** in the sampled azimuth range. Expected for an \(M_L\) 5.85 rupture (~4–5 km, ~2 s) inside a 3 s \(Pd\) window.

Direct regression of report-file \(Pd\) on \(|F_P|\) is weak (\(p=0.123\)); the statistical case for radiation therefore rests on **S-wave/PGA**, plus the +0.29 vs +0.28 match.

### Scientific meaning

**Bias term 2 is radiation-pattern sampling**, quantified. Directivity is not required. The +0.28 “geometry penalty” in Fig. 11 is the same number as this prediction.

## 中文

### 圖面內容（依稿件；根目錄無 `fig10.png`）

四聯圖檢驗方位結構是**輻射圖案**還是破裂**方向性**。

**(a)** RMT 機制的下半球 P 波輻射。灰點 166 站；紅星為首報三站，緊貼 **P 軸**（方位 293°）。三站平均 \(|F_P|=0.699\)，全方位平均 0.392，比值 1.78 → 預測規模偏差 \(+0.29\)（表 4）。

**(b)** \(|F_P|\)、\(|F_S|\) 對方位；45–180° 無站。

**(c)** 距離改正 PGA 對理論 \(|F_S|\)，擬合指數 **+1.13**（理論 1.0；\(p<0.001\)）。

**(d)** 去掉距離與輻射後，方位諧波振幅由 0.453 降到 **0.028**（\(p=0.92\)）：在已取樣方位內**沒有殘餘 directivity**。對 \(M_L\) 5.85、3 秒 \(Pd\) 窗是合理的。

報告檔 \(Pd\) 對 \(|F_P|\) 迴歸不顯著（\(p=0.123\)），輻射控制的統計證據主要在 **PGA／S 波**，再加上 +0.29 與 +0.28 吻合。

### 科學意義

**第二項偏差＝輻射圖案取樣**，可定量。不需要引入破裂方向性。圖 11 的 +0.28 幾何懲罰就是這個預測。
