# Figure 6. Intensity residual distributions (first vs second school report)

- **Image:** `fig06.png`
- **Section:** 4
- **Official caption:** Intensity residual (predicted minus observed) distributions for the first and second reports. The first report is entirely non-negative.

---

## English

### What the figure shows

Two histograms of **intensity error = predicted − observed** at 166 analyst-reviewed stations.

**First report** (13.67 s, \(M\) 6.6 / 30 km):

| Residual (ranks) | Share of stations |
| --- | --- |
| 0 (exact) | 10.2% |
| +1 | ~49% |
| +2 | ~25% |
| +3 and above | remainder |
| negative | **none** |

**Second report** (14.67 s, \(M\) 5.9 / 20 km): exact 27.1%; a few −1; still mostly +1. Later reports reach 80.7% within ±1 (Table 2).

### How to read it

Positive = **over-warning** (too high intensity). Zero = exact rank. The first histogram having no negative bar is the operational fact: **nobody was under-warned** on the public first product; most were over-warned by one to two ranks. Accuracy improves with updates, but PWS/TV do not send those updates.

### Scientific meaning

The \(M\) 6.6 overestimate is not an academic residual: it maps into **intensity over-alerting**. Combined with Fig. 3, the failure mode is conservative (extra counties, extra ranks), which is preferable to missed warning but costly for credibility if repeated.

## 中文

### 圖面內容

166 個分析師測站的**震度殘差（預測 − 實測）**直方圖。左：學校通道第 1 報（13.67 s，\(M\) 6.6／30 km）。右：第 2 報（14.67 s，\(M\) 5.9／20 km）。

### 如何判讀

正值＝**過度預警**（震度報太高）。0＝完全正確。首報**沒有負殘差**：公眾第一則沒有任何站被低估，多數高 1～2 級。後續更新準度上升（表 2 最終 ±1 達 80.7%），但 PWS／電視通常不發這些更新。

### 科學意義

\(M\) 6.6 高估會變成**震度多報**。搭配圖 3，失敗方向是保守（多縣市、多震級），比漏報好，但若反覆發生會消耗公信力。
