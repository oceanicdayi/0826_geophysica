# Figure 1. CWA real-time network and the Taitung 2026 offshore earthquake

- **Image:** `fig01.png` (also `../figures/manuscript/fig01.png` in the manuscript)
- **Section:** 1. Introduction
- **Official caption:** CWA real-time seismic network and the 25 August 2026 Taitung offshore earthquake. Grey circles: strong-motion stations; blue triangles: velocity/broadband stations, as configured in the eBEAR instance analysed here (551 and 228 stations respectively within the map area). Red star: the epicentre of the analysed event; the beachball, plotted offset and connected by a line to the epicentre, shows the Real-time Moment Tensor (RMT) focal mechanism used in Section 5 (strike 182°, dip 50°, rake 60°; Lee et al. 2014). Epicentres of the three comparison events of Section 6 are listed in Table 5.

## English

### What the figure shows

A map of Taiwan (~21–26°N, ~119–123.5°E) with the eBEAR real-time station set used for this study: 551 strong-motion sites (grey circles) and 228 velocity/broadband sites (blue triangles). The 25 August 2026 Taitung earthquake is a red star offshore of the southeast coast, labelled \(M_L\) 5.85, depth 13 km. An RMT beachball (strike/dip/rake 182°/50°/60°) is plotted offset and tied to the epicentre.

### How to read it

The land network is dense on the island and on Green Island / Lanyu, but **empty seaward of the east coast**. From the red star, every remaining station lies in a western–northwestern sector. That empty eastern half of the map *is* the azimuthal gap. Green Island is the nearest site (~14 km); an inland event of the same size would have a nearest station near 5 km and stations all around the source.

### Scientific meaning

This is the geometric premise of the paper. Offshore EEW must locate and size the source from a **one-sided, landward sample** of the wavefield. The beachball later explains why the first three magnitude stations sat on the P-wave radiation maximum (Section 5). Figure 1 does not yet show performance; it defines why later figures (gap, radiation, distance–magnitude coupling) are expected for this class of event.

## 中文

### 圖面內容

台灣及其週邊海域測站圖。灰點為強震站 551 站，藍三角為速度／寬頻站 228 站（本研究所用 eBEAR 實例）。紅星為 2026-08-25 臺東外海地震（\(M_L\) 5.85，深 13 km）。側移的沙灘球為中研院 RMT 震源機制（走向／傾角／滑動角 182°/50°/60°；Lee et al. 2014）。

### 如何判讀

島上測站很密，但**東岸以東海域沒有測站**。從紅星望去，其餘測站全在西～西北一側；東半側空白就是方位缺口。最近站在綠島（約 14 km）。同樣規模的內陸地震最近站約 5 km，且四面都有站。

### 科學意義

這張圖是全文的幾何前提：外海預警只能用**陸側單向波場**定位與定規模。沙灘球為第 5 節輻射圖案分析鋪路（首報三站落在 P 波輻射極大）。圖 1 不談快慢，只說明後面距離–規模耦合、方位取樣偏差為何必然出現。
