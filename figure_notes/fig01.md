## English

### What is drawn

A map of Taiwan and adjacent seas (~21–26°N, ~119–123.5°E). Grey circles are 551 strong-motion stations; blue triangles are 228 velocity/broadband stations in the eBEAR instance used here. Both families are dense on the main island; a few sites sit on Green Island, Lanyu, Penghu, and sparse offshore/OBC positions to the northeast. The red star is the 25 August 2026 Taitung epicentre (~22.63°N, 121.60°E), labelled \(M_L\) 5.85, 13 km. An RMT beachball is offset and tied to the star: reverse faulting with a strike-slip component, nodal plane 182°/50°/60° (Lee et al. 2014). A 100 km scale bar sits at lower left.

The three comparison epicentres of Section 6 (Yilan 2025, Hualien 2026a/b) are not plotted; they are given in Table 5.

### How to read it

Stand at the red star and look for stations. Every remaining station lies west to northwest. The empty half of the map east and south of the star *is* the azimuthal gap: there is no seaward coverage east of the east coast. Green Island is the nearest site (14.1 km). An inland earthquake of the same size would be surrounded, with a nearest station near 5 km (the Hualien events in Fig. 12).

The beachball is not decoration. Its P axis is nearly horizontal at azimuth 293°. That is the landward WNW sector that the first three magnitude stations occupy in Figs. 9–10.

### Scientific meaning

This figure is the geometric premise of the paper, not a performance plot. Offshore EEW must locate and size the source from a **one-sided, landward sample** of the wavefield. That geometry produces three later results: (i) early epicentres are pulled inland toward the station centroid (Fig. 4); (ii) assumed distance \(R\) is then too short, so \(M_{Pd}\) is too small until location improves (Fig. 8); (iii) the first magnitude stations sample one radiation lobe (Figs. 9–10). Classical azimuthal gap is necessarily large for any *correct* offshore location, so it cannot be used as a withhold-alert flag.

### Common misreading

Do not read the dense grey cloud as “good coverage for this event.” Coverage is good *on land*. For this source the network is one-sided. Also do not treat the beachball as the eBEAR solution; it is the Academia Sinica RMT used only in Section 5.

## 中文

### 圖面內容

台灣本島及週邊海域圖（約 21–26°N、119–123.5°E）。灰圈為 551 個強震站，藍三角為 228 個速度／寬頻站（本研究所用 eBEAR 實例）。測站在本島很密，綠島、蘭嶼、澎湖與東北外海有少量點。紅星為 2026-08-25 臺東外海震央（約 22.63°N、121.60°E），標 \(M_L\) 5.85、深 13 km。側移沙灘球以線連到紅星：逆斷層帶走向滑移分量，節面 182°/50°/60°（中研院 RMT；Lee et al. 2014）。左下有 100 km 比例尺。第 6 節三個對照事件震央未畫在圖上，列於表 5。

### 如何判讀

站在紅星上找測站：其餘站全在西～西北。星東、星南那半張空白就是方位缺口——東岸以東海域沒有測站。最近站在綠島（14.1 km）。同樣規模的內陸地震會被測站包圍，最近站約 5 km（圖 12 的花蓮事件）。

沙灘球不是裝飾。其 P 軸近水平、方位約 293°，正好是圖 9–10 首報三站所在的陸側 WNW 扇區。

### 科學意義

這是全文的幾何前提，不是效能圖。外海預警只能用**陸側單向波場**定位與定規模。後面三件事由此而來：（i）早期震央被拉向測站重心、落在陸上（圖 4）；（ii）假設距離 \(R\) 太短，\(M_{Pd}\) 偏低，位置改對才升高（圖 8）；（iii）首批規模站只採到一個輻射瓣（圖 9–10）。正確的外海解方位缺口必然大，因此缺口不能當「扣住警報」的旗標。

### 容易讀錯的地方

不要把陸上密密的灰點讀成「這次事件覆蓋很好」。覆蓋好的是陸地；對這個震源，測網是單側的。沙灘球也不是 eBEAR 的解，只是第 5 節用的中研院 RMT。
