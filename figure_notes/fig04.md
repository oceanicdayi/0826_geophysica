# Figure 4. Epicentre convergence of six parallel streams

- **Image:** `fig04.png`
- **Section:** 4
- **Official caption:** Epicentre convergence trajectories of the six parallel streams. The reference epicentre is the yellow star. Early geometric-centre solutions lie on land, 35–43 km northwest of the true offshore source.

---

## English

### What the figure shows

A zoomed map of the Taitung coast and Philippine Sea. The **yellow star** is the analyst reference epicentre (offshore). Coloured markers and dashed polylines are sequential epicentres from six eBEAR streams on four computers (geometric-centre `f42`/`f43` and Geiger `gei`). Time runs along each polyline from northwest (early) toward the star (later).

### How to read it

Early points sit **on or inland of the coast**, 35–43 km NW of the true source: the geometric-centre method returns the centroid of triggered land stations, so it cannot place the event at sea until the set of triggers changes. Geiger streams migrate offshore and cluster within ~1–5 km of the star (one Geiger first solution already 4.6 km). At least one geometric-centre stream **never recovers** (final inland error ~36 km).

### Scientific meaning

This is the **distance term** in pictures: assumed \(R\) starts too small, so \(M_{Pd}\) starts too low for badly inland solutions, then jumps when location moves offshore (Fig. 5a, Fig. 8b). It also motivates the **cross-stream consistency** gate: at the first-alert second, streams on the same computer can differ by tens of kilometres (Table 3: Geiger vs centroid). Agreement among independent location families is a better promotion test than azimuthal gap, which improves *spuriously* when the epicentre is dragged inland.

## 中文

### 圖面內容

臺東沿岸放大圖。黃星為分析師參考震央（外海）。六條 eBEAR 串流（幾何中心 `f42`/`f43` 與 Geiger `gei`）的震央軌跡由西北（早期）走向黃星（後期）。

### 如何判讀

早期點在**岸上或內陸**，距真震源西北 35–43 km：幾何中心法取觸發陸站的重心，在觸發組合改變前無法把事件放到海上。Geiger 串流會往外海收斂到黃星附近 1–5 km（有一條首解已 4.6 km）。至少一條幾何中心串流**始終回不來**（最終內陸誤差約 36 km）。

### 科學意義

這是**距離項**的地圖版：假設 \(R\) 一開始太短，錯在陸上的解 \(M_{Pd}\) 會偏低；震央移到外海後規模跳升（圖 5a、圖 8b）。也支持**跨串流定位一致性**閘門：首報那一秒，同一機器上兩套算法可差數十公里。獨立定位族是否一致，比方位缺口更適合當發布條件——缺口在震央被拖進陸地網時會「假改善」。
