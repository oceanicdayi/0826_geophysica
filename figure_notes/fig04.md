# Figure 4. Epicentre convergence of six parallel streams

- **Image:** `fig04.png`
- **Section:** 4
- **Official caption:** Epicentre convergence trajectories of the six parallel streams. The reference epicentre is the yellow star. Early geometric-centre solutions lie on land, 35–43 km northwest of the true offshore source.

---

## English

### What is drawn

A zoomed map of the Taitung coast and Philippine Sea (~22.2–23.0°N, ~121.2–122.0°E). The **yellow star** is the analyst reference epicentre (22.6252°N, 121.5968°E). Coloured markers linked by dashed polylines are sequential epicentres from six eBEAR streams. On the PNG, circles are geometric-centre (centroid) solutions and triangles are Geiger inversions; the legend still uses internal host labels. Time runs along each polyline from northwest (early) toward the star (later).

### How to read it

Early points sit **on or inland of the coast**, 35–43 km northwest of the true source. That is not a random scatter: the geometric-centre method returns the centroid of triggered *land* stations, so it cannot place the event at sea until the trigger set changes. Geiger streams migrate offshore and cluster within ~1–5 km of the star; one Geiger first solution is already 4.6 km away. At least one geometric-centre stream **never recovers** (final inland error ~36 km). All tracks that do recover move **southeast**, from the network toward the sea — the opposite of a random walk.

Final Geiger errors in the text are 0.8–1.9 km for the four converging streams.

### Scientific meaning

This is the **distance term** as a map. Assumed hypocentral distance \(R\) starts too small for inland solutions, so \(M_{Pd}\) starts too *low* (Fig. 8a: 5.66). When the epicentre jumps offshore, \(R\) lengthens and \(M_{Pd}\) jumps *up* into the overestimate (6.55). The figure also motivates the **cross-stream location-consistency** gate: at the first-alert second, a Geiger stream and a centroid stream on the same feed differed by tens of kilometres (Table 3). Independent methods agreeing is a better promotion test than azimuthal gap, which improves *spuriously* when the epicentre is dragged inland (Fig. 9).

### Common misreading

Inland early points are not “the earthquake was on land.” They are the station centroid. Also, smaller \(R\) **lowers** \(M_{Pd}\) in the operational formula \(M_{Pd}=4.79+1.16\log_{10}(Pd)+1.85\log_{10}(R)\); it does not raise it. The first public \(M_{Pd}\) 6.6 came from a *well-located* Geiger solution, not from the inland centroid.

## 中文

### 圖面內容

臺東沿岸與菲律賓海放大圖。**黃星**為分析師參考震央（22.6252°N、121.5968°E）。彩色點與虛線折線是六條 eBEAR 串流的連續震央。圖例上圓點為幾何中心（測站重心）解，三角為 Geiger 反演；PNG 仍標內部主機名稱。時間沿折線由西北（早期）走向黃星（後期）。

### 如何判讀

早期點在**岸上或內陸**，距真震源西北 35–43 km。這不是亂散：幾何中心法取觸發**陸站**的重心，觸發組合改變前無法把事件放到海上。Geiger 串流往外海收斂到黃星附近 1–5 km，有一條首解已 4.6 km。至少一條幾何中心串流**始終回不來**（最終內陸誤差約 36 km）。會收斂的軌跡一律**往東南**、從測網走向海——不是隨機遊走。正文中四條收斂串流最終誤差 0.8–1.9 km。

### 科學意義

這是**距離項**的地圖版。陸上解的假設 \(R\) 太短，\(M_{Pd}\) 會*偏低*（圖 8a：5.66）。震央一跳到外海，\(R\) 變長，\(M_{Pd}\) 反而*升高*成高估（6.55）。也支持**跨串流定位一致性**閘門：首報那一秒，同一資料流上 Geiger 與重心可差數十公里（表 3）。獨立算法是否一致，比方位缺口更適合當發布條件——缺口在震央被拖進陸地時會「假改善」（圖 9）。

### 容易讀錯的地方

早期陸上點不是「地震在陸上」，那是測站重心。\(R\) 變小會依作業公式**壓低** \(M_{Pd}\)，不會抬高。公眾看到的 \(M_{Pd}\) 6.6 來自**定位好的** Geiger 解，不是內陸重心解。
