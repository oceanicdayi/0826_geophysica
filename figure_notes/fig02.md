# Figure 2. PWS coverage and S-wave response-time rings

- **Image:** `fig02.png`
- **Section:** 4
- **Official caption:** PWS coverage of the first alert (17 counties, shaded) with the epicentre (star) and dashed circles showing the S-wave response-time contours at 10 s intervals from the alert instant. The 0 s contour marks the theoretical blind zone boundary.

---

## English

### What is drawn

A Taiwan map titled on the PNG as PWS message coverage with S-wave response-time rings for this event. The red star is the offshore epicentre. Yellow fill marks the **17 counties/cities** named in the first Public Warning System (PWS) cell-broadcast. Northern counties (Greater Taipei, Taoyuan, Keelung and neighbours) remain white: they were not in this broadcast. Dashed concentric circles, centred on the epicentre, are labelled 0, 10, 20, … 70 s.

### How to read it

The rings are **not** travel-time from origin. Zero is the **alert instant** (13.67 s after origin). The 0 s circle is where the S wave had already arrived when the product left the warning system; inside it is the theoretical **blind zone**. Each next ring is 10 s of extra S-wave lead.

On this map the 0 s ring lies mostly at sea and clips Green Island / the Taitung coast. Taitung City (48.7 km) has only ~2.1 s theoretical S lead. Western and northern yellow counties fall in the 20–40 s belts.

Yellow is the **predicted** footprint at the first-alert source (\(M_{Pd}\) 6.6, depth 30 km), not the observed intensity-3 map. Observed intensity ≥ 3 reached 15 counties (Fig. 3), so yellow is slightly larger than the true intensity-3 area.

### Scientific meaning

The first public product was **fast and spatially conservative**: 17 counties warned versus 15 that later reached intensity 3. Over-coverage is the spatial counterpart of the \(M_{Pd}\) 6.6 overestimate — the preferred failure direction (over-alert, not miss). The 0 s ring shows the limit that software cannot remove: for an offshore source the closest exposed community is also the least protected.

### Common misreading

White counties are not “the system failed there.” They were below the PWS intensity threshold on the first (and only) cell-broadcast. PWS does not send updates; the yellow map is what the public keeps.

## 中文

### 圖面內容

臺灣地圖。紅星為外海震央。黃色為首報 PWS 細胞廣播納入的 **17 縣市**。北部（大臺北、桃園、基隆一帶）留白，未進這則廣播。以震央為心的虛線圓標 0、10、20…70 秒。

### 如何判讀

這些圓**不是**從發震起算的走時。0 秒是**警報發出時刻**（發震後 13.67 秒）。0 秒圓是警報離開系統時 S 波已走到的位置；圓內為理論**盲區**。往外每圈多 10 秒 S 波領先。

本圖 0 秒圓大半在海上，擦到綠島／臺東沿岸。臺東市（48.7 km）理論 S 波領先僅約 2.1 秒。西部、北部黃色縣市多落在 20–40 秒帶。

黃色是首報震源（\(M_{Pd}\) 6.6、深 30 km）的**預測**範圍，不是實測震度 3 圖。實測震度 ≥ 3 為 15 縣市（圖 3），故黃色略大於真實震度 3 區。

### 科學意義

首報**夠快、空間上偏保守**：17 縣市收到警報，後來達到震度 3 的是 15 縣市。多蓋的縣市對應 \(M_{Pd}\) 6.6 高估——失敗方向是多報，不是漏報。0 秒圓標出軟體消不掉的上限：外海事件最近的暴露聚落也是最沒保護的。

### 容易讀錯的地方

留白縣市不是「那裡系統失敗」，只是未達該則 PWS 震度門檻。PWS 通常每事件只發一次，黃色就是公眾拿到、且不會被更新的範圍。
