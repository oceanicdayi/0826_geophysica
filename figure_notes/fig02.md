# Figure 2. PWS coverage and S-wave response-time rings

- **Image:** `fig02.png`
- **Section:** 4
- **Official caption:** PWS coverage of the first alert (17 counties, shaded) with the epicentre (star) and dashed circles showing the S-wave response-time contours at 10 s intervals from the alert instant. The 0 s contour marks the theoretical blind zone boundary.

---

## English

### What the figure shows

Taiwan shaded by whether each county was in the first Public Warning System (PWS) cell-broadcast. Yellow = included (17 counties/cities). A red star marks the offshore epicentre. Dashed circles are **S-wave response-time contours** at 10 s steps, timed from the alert instant (13.67 s after origin), not from origin time.

### How to read it

- The **0 s ring** is where the S wave had already arrived *when the alert left the system*. Inside it is the theoretical **blind zone**: shaking starts before or with the alert.
- Rings at 10, 20, … s are extra lead time for S relative to that instant. Western and northern Taiwan typically fall in the 20–40 s belts on this map.
- Coverage (yellow) is **predicted** footprint at the first-alert magnitude/depth (\(M\) 6.6 / 30 km), not the observed intensity-3 map. Observed intensity 3 reached 15 counties (Table 1 / Fig. 3), so yellow is slightly larger than the true felt area of intensity 3.

### Scientific meaning

Operationally the first alert was **fast and slightly conservative in space**: 17 counties warned vs 15 that later reached intensity 3. The 0 s ring shows that Taitung City and the near-source coast/islands have almost no S-wave lead (Taitung City ~2.1 s). The over-coverage is the spatial counterpart of the \(M\) 6.6 overestimate: extra counties are the preferred failure direction (over-alert, not miss).

## 中文

### 圖面內容

首報 PWS 細胞廣播覆蓋圖。黃色為納入警報的 17 縣市；紅星為外海震央。虛線圓為**以警報發出時刻為 0** 的 S 波剩餘時間等值線，間隔 10 秒。

### 如何判讀

- **0 秒圓**：警報離開系統時 S 波已走到的位置；圓內為理論**盲區**（搖晃已到或同時到）。
- 10、20…秒圓：該帶相對警報還有多少 S 波領先時間。本圖西部、北部多落在 20–40 秒帶。
- 黃色是首報規模／深度（\(M\) 6.6／30 km）的**預測**範圍，不是實測震度 3 圖。實測震度 3 為 15 縣市，故黃色略大於真實有感範圍。

### 科學意義

首報**夠快、空間上略偏保守**：17 縣市收到警報，後來達到震度 3 的是 15 縣市。0 秒圓顯示臺東市與近源海岸／離島幾乎沒有 S 波領先（臺東市約 2.1 秒）。多蓋的縣市對應 \(M\) 6.6 高估：失敗方向是多報，不是漏報。
